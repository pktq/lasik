# Disclaimer

LASIK is a static deobfuscator and prompt-injection filter. It removes protection that BLA (Blind LLM Agents) adds to software so that people and AI agents can analyse it.

**Use it only where you have the right to analyse the software**, for example:

- software you or your organisation own or build;
- malware, phishing kits and other threat samples, for defence and research;
- CTFs and public challenges that invite analysis, such as BLA's own CrackMes;
- code you are contractually or legally authorised to audit.

Removing or bypassing protection on software you do not have rights to may breach its licence, terms of service, or laws such as the US DMCA (17 U.S.C. §1201), the EU Software Directive, or computer-misuse statutes. You are responsible for how you use LASIK.

LASIK does not execute the code it analyses, does not contact the network, and does not publish keys, flags or secrets it uncovers. Healed output is provided as-is, without warranty that it is behaviourally identical to the input in every environment; see the "Honest limits" section of the README.

LASIK is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the BLA or REA projects.
