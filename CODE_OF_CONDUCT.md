# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcoming and constructive; no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or publishing others' private information. Maintainers may remove contributions and restrict participation to uphold this.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, open an issue at <https://github.com/pktq/lasik/issues> (ask for a private channel if the matter is sensitive and a maintainer will arrange one), or use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/pktq/lasik/security/advisories/new) if the matter cannot be public. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the [Enforcement Guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of the Contributor Covenant: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
